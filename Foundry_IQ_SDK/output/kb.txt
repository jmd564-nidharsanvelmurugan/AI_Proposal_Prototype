# Problem Statement

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC currently faces limitations in the granularity and integration of its pipeline and customer acquisition reporting. The reliance on multiple data sources—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and soon QuickBooks—means that linking lead creation through to assets under management (AUM) is both complex and fragmented. Present processes lack a unified data model for Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, resulting in restricted business visibility and the inability to adequately trace marketing investments through to actual client value and ROI.

This technical fragmentation has clear business consequences. Without an integrated reporting suite capable of automated data ingestion, transformation, and visualization, PURE’s leadership faces delays in obtaining actionable insights. Key metrics such as lead pipeline velocity, CAC, and L2A conversion rates are difficult to measure at the required granularity, hampering real-time decision-making. The lack of robust, scalable, and automated reporting not only constrains PURE’s internal management capabilities but could also undermine investor confidence and readiness for buy- and sell-side diligence. # Approach

The engagement for PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is tailored to address the specific challenges and ambitions of a leading B2C wealth management firm supported by PE ownership. Recognizing PURE’s established technology environment—including Salesforce, Tamarac, Microsoft Fabric, and Power BI—the approach is calibrated to maximize existing investments while introducing rigor, automation, and scalability. The methodology directly targets the client’s need for granular L2A and CAC insights, rapid integration of new data sources, and alignment to best practices for scalable, PE-backed analytics platforms.

A structured, phased methodology will be adopted, reflecting proven frameworks from the knowledge base for financial services data platform modernization. Each phase provides a controlled stage gate for risk management, stakeholder validation, and capability transfer. The project is divided into: Discovery & Data Assessment, Solution Architecture & Mockups, Blueprint & Roadmapping, and Knowledge Transfer & Change Management. This alignment ensures that each deliverable is actionable, measurable, and future-proof, while risks related to data integration or source readiness are actively managed at every step【4:1†source】【4:21†source】.

**Phase 1: Discovery & Data Assessment, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 1–3**

**Summary:** Establish a shared understanding of data assets, current infrastructure, and business requirements for L2A and CAC reporting. The outcome is a validated current-state baseline and risk register.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to confirm reporting objectives, user personas, and KPI priorities.
- Review and map all in-scope systems (Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, Azure) and available data fields.
- Assess data quality, completeness, and integration challenges, specifically for CAC-critical fields.
- Identify coverage gaps, data inconsistencies, and required remediations.
- Map out reporting pain points and operational constraints with business teams.

**Phase 2: Solution Architecture & Power BI Mockups, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 4–5**

**Summary:** Develop and validate technical architecture and dashboard mockups to meet business requirements and align on the end-state solution vision.

**Activities:**
- Design logical and physical data models for unified L2A and CAC reporting.
- Develop wireframes/mockups for eight Power BI dashboards, with focus group feedback sessions.
- Define calculation logic, KPIs, and drill-down structures, validated against PE playbook standards.
- Architect batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines (Azure Fabric).
- Draft technical solution architecture documentation and data quality playbooks.

**Phase 3: Data Gap Remediation & Integration Readiness, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 6–7**

**Summary:** Resolve identified data quality and integration gaps, and prepare systems for seamless data ingestion and automated reporting.

**Activities:**
- Implement required mappings, cleansing logic, and validations for key data sources.
- Build automated data pipelines for QuickBooks and Salesforce integrations.
- Test and document end-to-end data flow, with data lineage visibility.
- Finalize data quality remediation plan and produce readiness sign-off.
- Conduct incremental user testing for data integrity and completeness.

**Phase 4: Build Roadmap & Capability Transfer, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 8–19**

**Summary:** Deliver a detailed implementation plan for the full build phase, ensuring roles, milestones, and resource needs are clear, and support transition to internal ownership.

**Activities:**
- Develop work breakdown structures, timelines, milestones, and risk dependencies for Phase 3B.
- Align resource plans and clearly define roles/responsibilities between internal and external teams.
- Deliver knowledge transfer and training on new reports, data assets, and governance processes.
- Set up feedback and validation sessions with leadership and end users.
- Iterate on all documentation and solution artifacts based on stakeholder input.

This phased, iterative approach reduces delivery risk by ensuring feedback and course correction at each stage, drives early value through quick wins (mockups and data assessments), and aligns with PURE’s operational imperatives for scalable, automated reporting. By closely coordinating with PURE’s leadership, technical, and marketing teams, the methodology ensures outcomes are owned, actionable, and strategically relevant.
