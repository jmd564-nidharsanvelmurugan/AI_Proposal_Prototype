┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    LOGICAL PROGRESSION OF A PROPOSAL                         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Business Context      → "Where are we now?" (Current state)
         │
         ▼
2. Problem Statement     → "What's wrong?" (Pain points)
         │
         ▼
3. Objectives            → "Where do we want to go?" (Desired future)
         │
         ▼
4. Deliverables          → "What will we produce?" (Tangible outputs)
         │
         ▼
5. Approach              → "How will we get there?" (Methodology)
         │
         ▼
6. Outcomes              → "What will happen?" (Results of deliverables)
         │
         ▼
7. Business Impact       → "What is it worth?" (ROI & value)





















Dependency Strength
Dependency	Strength	Explanation
2 → 1	 🔗 Strong	Cannot identify problems without understanding context
3 → 1,2	 🔗 Strong	Objectives are meaningless without current state and problems
4 → 3	 🔗 Strong	Deliverables directly serve objectives
5 → 3,4	 🔗 Strong	Approach designed to create deliverables and achieve objectives
6 → 3,4,5	🔗 Strong	Outcomes result from executing approach to create deliverables
7 → 6	 🔗 Strong	Financial impact calculated from outcomes
