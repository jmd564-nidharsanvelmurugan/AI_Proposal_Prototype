# Approach

The engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** is designed to directly address the firm’s need for enhanced visibility into its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s growth depends on understanding the full customer journey from lead generation to AUM realization. This approach leverages the existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse data platforms while integrating QuickBooks and Salesforce to deliver a unified, automated reporting suite that provides granular insights into marketing ROI and pipeline efficiency.

Our structured, phased methodology ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives and technical environment. Each phase builds progressively—from data access and discovery through model design and implementation planning—allowing for iterative validation with stakeholders and seamless integration into existing systems. This disciplined approach ensures that the solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with Pure’s long‑term vision for scalable, data‑driven decision‑making.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Data Access, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish foundational access, documentation, and stakeholder alignment to enable smooth execution of subsequent phases.

**Activities:**  
- Coordinate with Pure and Lee Equity Partners to confirm system access and permissions for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and Power BI.  
- Gather existing documentation on data architecture, infrastructure, and reporting processes.  
- Schedule workshops and define meeting cadence with data, technology, and marketing teams.  
- Validate reconciliation sources and ensure alignment with existing reporting outputs.  
- Confirm engagement scope, dependencies, and success criteria for Phase 3A and 3B timelines.

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Initial Model Design, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 2 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Develop a clear understanding of current data sources and initiate design of the target‑state L2A and CAC data model.

**Activities:**  
- Review data sources across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Conduct a data gap assessment to identify missing or low‑quality data impacting CAC reporting.  
- Map data flows and define relationships between lead, client, and AUM entities.  
- Draft initial KPI calculation logic for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Identify “quick wins” for early reporting improvements and prioritize use cases by business impact.

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Finalization & Mock‑Up Development, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 4**

**Summary:** Finalize the end‑state data model and develop Power BI mock‑ups to visualize priority reporting use cases.

**Activities:**  
- Refine KPI logic and metric definitions based on stakeholder feedback.  
- Design and iterate Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) for L2A and CAC dashboards.  
- Validate mock‑ups with senior leadership to ensure clarity and actionable insights.  
- Align data model structure with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse architecture.  
- Document data transformation requirements for automated ingestion pipelines.

---

**Phase 4: Data Quality & Integration Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**

**Summary:** Define the approach for data cleaning, enrichment, and integration to support accurate and automated reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Review results from the data gap assessment and recommend remediation strategies.  
- Define integration approach for QuickBooks ingestion into the Fabric platform.  
- Establish data quality rules and validation checkpoints for L2A and CAC datasets.  
- Identify dependencies and sequencing for Phase 3B implementation.  
- Document technical recommendations for data enrichment and transformation automation.

---

**Phase 5: Implementation Planning & Roadmap Development, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 6**

**Summary:** Develop the detailed implementation roadmap and technical approach for Phase 3B execution.

**Activities:**  
- Consolidate findings into a comprehensive Phase 3B Implementation Plan.  
- Define build sequencing for automated pipelines and Power BI report deployment.  
- Outline resource requirements and timeline dependencies for QuickBooks integration.  
- Finalize deliverables including KPI Book, Data Model Design, and Technical Approach documentation.  
- Present roadmap and recommendations to Pure’s leadership for approval and next‑phase initiation.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes aligned with stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon validated insights, enabling Pure to achieve a scalable, automated, and insight‑driven reporting environment that supports continued growth and data maturity.
