# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact for **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** by establishing a unified Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework that enhances visibility into marketing performance and pipeline efficiency. The solution will enable data‑driven decision‑making for leadership and investment stakeholders, supporting Pure’s B2C growth strategy and private equity objectives through improved reporting accuracy, automation, and scalability.  

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** A unified data model will provide end‑to‑end transparency into the client journey from lead generation through asset acquisition, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize marketing strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Insights:** Integrated CAC reporting will quantify marketing efficiency and ROI, allowing the business to allocate spend more effectively and improve profitability across acquisition channels.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual effort, improve data accuracy, and ensure timely delivery of insights through Power BI dashboards.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enhanced analytics will link marketing activities directly to AUM outcomes, empowering stakeholders to evaluate campaign performance and refine investment in lead generation.  
- **Data‑Driven Decision Enablement:** Consistent, trusted metrics across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Azure Fabric will strengthen executive confidence in data‑led decisions and support long‑term scalability of reporting capabilities.  
- **Reduced Operational Risk from Data Gaps:** Addressing data quality and integration dependencies will mitigate reporting inconsistencies, ensuring leadership maintains clear visibility into pipeline performance and business growth levers.
