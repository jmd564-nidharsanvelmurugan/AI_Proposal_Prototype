# Approach

The engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) pipeline. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s existing data ecosystem—spanning Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI—provides a strong foundation for advanced reporting. This initiative builds upon that infrastructure to design and implement a unified data model and reporting suite that connects marketing performance to asset growth, enabling data‑driven decision‑making and improved ROI tracking.

Our structured, phased methodology ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities and technical environment. Each phase builds logically on the previous one—from discovery and data assessment through model design and implementation planning—providing clear checkpoints for stakeholder validation and technical readiness. This approach balances speed and rigor, ensuring that the final solution is both scalable and deeply integrated with Pure’s existing Microsoft‑based data platform.

---

**Phase 3A: Design & Discovery, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the L2A and CAC reporting solution through data access, source assessment, and initial model and dashboard design.

**Activities:**  
- Secure access to Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and QuickBooks environments.  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm reporting requirements and KPI definitions.  
- Perform data source review and gap assessment to identify missing or low‑quality data impacting CAC and pipeline reporting.  
- Develop initial target‑state data model and define KPI calculation logic for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Create high‑level Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority reporting use cases.  
- Document technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation Planning & Technical Design, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Finalize the end‑state data model, confirm technical design, and develop a detailed implementation roadmap for the build phase.

**Activities:**  
- Refine and finalize the L2A and CAC data model based on stakeholder feedback and mock‑up validation.  
- Define data cleaning and enrichment recommendations informed by the data gap assessment.  
- Develop detailed technical specifications for automated batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Validate integration approach for QuickBooks and Salesforce data ingestion and transformation.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining sequencing, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Deliver the KPI Book, finalized Power BI mock‑ups, and technical documentation for build readiness.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing from discovery through design and implementation planning, the engagement provides a robust foundation for scalable, automated, and insight‑driven reporting aligned with stakeholder expectations and long‑term growth objectives.
