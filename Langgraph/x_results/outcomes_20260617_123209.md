# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact for **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** by enabling data‑driven visibility into marketing performance and client acquisition efficiency. Focused on developing a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite, the initiative will empower leadership and marketing teams to make informed, ROI‑based decisions aligned with the firm’s B2C growth strategy and private equity stakeholder objectives.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Establishment of a unified L2A data model will provide end‑to‑end transparency into the client journey from lead generation through asset conversion, enabling precise measurement of conversion performance.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Insights:** Integration of QuickBooks and marketing data will deliver reliable CAC metrics, allowing leadership to assess marketing efficiency and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Automated and Scalable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and scalable reporting outputs that reduce manual effort and reporting lag.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enhanced Power BI dashboards will translate complex data into actionable insights, enabling data‑driven evaluation of campaign effectiveness and return on marketing investment.  
- **Strengthened Data‑Driven Decision Making:** Consolidated and accurate reporting will empower senior leadership and investment partners to make strategic growth decisions based on verified performance metrics.  
- **Reduced Operational Risk from Data Gaps:** Addressing data quality and integration dependencies will mitigate reporting inconsistencies, ensuring confidence in business performance insights and compliance with internal governance standards.
