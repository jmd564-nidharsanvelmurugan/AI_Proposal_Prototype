# Understanding

- The client seeks to address limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting and the absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.  
- Current reporting does not provide sufficient visibility into the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey, limiting understanding of marketing effectiveness.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating gaps in CAC data and hindering unified financial reporting.  
- The organization requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model to capture and analyze lead progression and acquisition costs more accurately.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent reporting refreshes.  
- The system must support multiple user groups, including data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams, each requiring tailored access to insights.  
- Integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks is mandatory to enable end‑to‑end visibility of lead, financial, and performance data.  
- Security and compliance requirements are not specified, indicating a need for further clarification during subsequent phases.
