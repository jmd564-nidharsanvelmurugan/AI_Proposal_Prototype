# Outcomes

This engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by transforming how the firm tracks and interprets its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. Focused on pipeline analytics within a SaaS‑based financial application, the initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C growth model and private‑equity stakeholder objectives to improve marketing ROI and data‑driven decision making.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility and Marketing ROI Insight:** Development of the L2A and CAC reporting suite will provide leadership with detailed visibility into the customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management, enabling precise evaluation of marketing effectiveness.  
- **Automated and Accurate Reporting Processes:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and error‑free reporting across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and other core systems.  
- **Improved Decision‑Making Through Actionable KPIs:** Establishment of clearly defined L2A conversion and CAC metrics will allow senior management to make informed strategic decisions on resource allocation and campaign optimization.  
- **Enhanced Data Quality and Integration Confidence:** Integration of QuickBooks and assessment of data quality will strengthen the reliability of financial and marketing data, reducing manual reconciliation and improving trust in analytics outputs.  
- **Scalable Data Platform for Future Growth:** The enhanced data model and reporting framework will be designed to scale with Pure’s expanding data sources and reporting needs, supporting long‑term data maturity and operational efficiency.  
- **Reduced Risk of Marketing Inefficiency:** By addressing current visibility gaps, the solution mitigates the risk of underperforming campaigns and misaligned spend, ensuring marketing investments deliver measurable returns.
