# Business Context

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is a U.S.-based financial advisory and wealth management firm providing financial advisory services and asset management to individual clients. Operating under a B2C model, the firm’s growth is supported by its partnership with Lee Equity Partners and managed through key stakeholders including Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager. The organization leverages a modern Microsoft data environment, including Azure Synapse, Microsoft Fabric, and Power BI, alongside core systems such as Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks.

The firm is undertaking this initiative to enhance its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Current reporting provides limited granularity and lacks integrated insights into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance. PURE seeks to design and implement a comprehensive L2A and CAC data model, supported by automated data ingestion and transformation processes, to deliver accurate, scalable, and actionable reporting. This effort will enable leadership to better understand the journey from lead generation to managed assets and evaluate return on marketing spend, ultimately driving greater data maturity and informed decision-making across the business.
