# Outcomes

This engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will deliver measurable business impact by transforming how marketing and pipeline performance are understood and managed. Focused on the development of a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite, the project aligns with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and supports leadership and PE stakeholders in achieving data‑driven growth and ROI transparency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility:** Establishment of a unified L2A data model will provide end‑to‑end transparency across the client acquisition funnel, enabling leadership to pinpoint conversion drivers and optimize marketing investments.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will produce reliable CAC metrics, allowing Pure to assess marketing efficiency and allocate spend toward the highest‑return channels.  
- **Automated and Scalable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely reporting and scalability for future data sources and business growth.  
- **Improved Decision‑Making and ROI Tracking:** Enhanced Power BI dashboards will deliver actionable insights into lead conversion and marketing performance, empowering executives to make informed, data‑backed strategic decisions.  
- **Reduced Operational Risk from Data Gaps:** Addressing data quality and integration dependencies will mitigate reporting inaccuracies, ensuring confidence in metrics used for performance evaluation and investment decisions.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Governance:** The new reporting framework will advance Pure’s data maturity, supporting long‑term scalability and alignment with PE‑level transparency and performance oversight.
