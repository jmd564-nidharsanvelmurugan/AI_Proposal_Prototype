# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting outputs.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey—from lead generation through asset acquisition—to inform strategic marketing and growth decisions.  
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.  
- Strengthen pipeline and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to support data-driven decision-making and operational efficiency.  
- Build a scalable data foundation capable of integrating future data sources and supporting long-term reporting maturity across the organization.
