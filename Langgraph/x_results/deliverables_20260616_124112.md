# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data sources.  

- Alignment of KPIs with business objectives to ensure consistent measurement across marketing and pipeline performance.  

- Inclusion of dimension dictionary detailing segmentation attributes for slicing and analyzing KPIs.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑Ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard mock‑ups illustrating layout, visual hierarchy, and user navigation for L2A and CAC reporting.  

- Representation of key insights and KPIs to guide dashboard development in the subsequent build phase.  

- Validation sessions with stakeholders to confirm usability and alignment with reporting needs.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema outlining entities, relationships, and data flows supporting L2A and CAC reporting within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Documentation of data transformation logic and integration points with Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks.  

- Technical specifications enabling scalable, automated ingestion and refresh processes.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Evaluation of data completeness, quality, and readiness across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  

- Identification of missing or inconsistent data elements impacting KPI accuracy.  

- Recommendations for remediation and data quality improvement to support reliable reporting.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the proposed architecture and integration strategy, including QuickBooks ingestion and transformation pipelines.  

- Definition of batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric to automate data movement and reporting refreshes.  

- Outline of dependencies, control points, and validation steps to ensure robust implementation.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and timelines for the build and deployment of the L2A and CAC reporting suite.  

- Sequencing of tasks across data integration, model development, and Power BI dashboard implementation.  

- Identification of resource requirements, dependencies, and risk mitigation measures to ensure successful execution.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
