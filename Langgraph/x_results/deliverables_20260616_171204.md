# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC**, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

---

**1. KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, formulas, and data sources.  

- Alignment of KPIs with business objectives to ensure consistent measurement across marketing and pipeline performance.  

- Inclusion of a dimension dictionary outlining key data attributes and hierarchies for standardized reporting.  

---

**2. Power BI Mock‑Ups (up to 8 Views)**  
- Design of interactive dashboard mock‑ups illustrating the structure, layout, and visualizations for L2A and CAC reporting.  

- Representation of key user journeys and insights to guide the final Power BI build phase.  

- Incorporation of feedback from data, marketing, and leadership teams to refine usability and clarity.  

---

**3. Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema design for the L2A and CAC data model, defining entities, relationships, and data flows across Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks.  

- Logical and physical data model documentation to support scalable integration within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Mapping of data lineage and transformation logic to ensure traceability and data quality.  

---

**4. Data Gap Assessment**  
- Evaluation of current data completeness, consistency, and quality across all relevant systems.  

- Identification of missing or misaligned data elements impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  

- Recommendations for remediation actions and data governance improvements.  

---

**5. Technical Approach Document**  
- Comprehensive description of the proposed architecture, integration methods, and automation strategy for batch data processing.  

- Detailed plan for QuickBooks data ingestion and transformation within the existing Azure Fabric environment.  

- Definition of technical dependencies, assumptions, and control points to ensure robust implementation.  

---

**6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Phased roadmap outlining key milestones, timelines, and resource requirements for the build and deployment phase.  

- Sequencing of activities for data integration, model deployment, and Power BI report development.  

- Risk and dependency matrix to manage inter‑system coordination and ensure timely delivery.  

---

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC**’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
