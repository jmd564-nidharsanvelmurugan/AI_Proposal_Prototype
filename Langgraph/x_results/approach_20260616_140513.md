# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC operates within the financial advisory and wealth management industry, serving clients across the United States through a B2C model. This engagement is designed to address Pure’s specific need for enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. The approach focuses on integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data to create a unified, automated, and scalable reporting environment that delivers granular insights into marketing ROI and pipeline performance.  

Our methodology follows a structured, phased framework that ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities, technical landscape, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—progressing from discovery and data understanding to model design, mock‑up development, and implementation planning—ensuring that the final solution is both technically robust and business‑aligned.  

---

**Phase 1: Discovery and Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 1**  

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the engagement by confirming access, aligning stakeholders, and defining the scope and success criteria.  

**Activities:**  
- Confirm project scope, objectives, and deliverables with key stakeholders (Jason Carver, Lee Equity Partners).  
- Secure access to Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks environments.  
- Review existing documentation on data architecture, infrastructure, and reporting.  
- Define meeting cadence, communication plan, and governance structure.  
- Validate dependencies, constraints, and data access requirements for subsequent phases.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment and Gap Analysis, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 2 to Week 3**  

**Summary:** Gain a comprehensive understanding of current data sources and identify data quality or availability gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Conduct a detailed review of Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data structures.  
- Map existing data flows and assess integration readiness for QuickBooks ingestion.  
- Evaluate data quality, completeness, and reconciliation across systems.  
- Identify and document data gaps and dependencies affecting KPI calculation.  
- Prioritize data remediation needs and define recommendations for Phase 3B implementation.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Design and KPI Definition, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 4**  

**Summary:** Develop the target‑state data model and define the logic for L2A and CAC KPIs to support consistent, accurate reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Design the conceptual and logical data model for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Define KPI calculation logic, including formulas, data lineage, and source mappings.  
- Validate KPI definitions and data relationships with business and technical stakeholders.  
- Align data model design with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse architecture standards.  
- Document data model specifications for technical build and reporting integration.  

---

**Phase 4: Reporting Mock‑Ups and Validation, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**  

**Summary:** Translate the data model and KPI definitions into visual Power BI mock‑ups to validate reporting requirements and user experience.  

**Activities:**  
- Develop up to eight Power BI mock‑ups focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Incorporate stakeholder feedback to refine dashboard layout and visual hierarchy.  
- Ensure alignment of visuals with KPI Book definitions and business logic.  
- Validate usability and interpretability with marketing and leadership teams.  
- Finalize mock‑ups for inclusion in the Phase 3B implementation plan.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach and Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 6**  

**Summary:** Finalize the technical design, data integration approach, and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation.  

**Activities:**  
- Confirm end‑state data model and integration design based on prior phase outputs.  
- Define technical approach for QuickBooks ingestion and automated batch processing.  
- Develop detailed implementation roadmap, including sequencing, dependencies, and resource needs.  
- Provide recommendations for data cleaning, enrichment, and governance alignment.  
- Deliver the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, consolidating all design and planning artifacts.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing methodically from discovery through design and planning, the engagement minimizes risk, maximizes stakeholder alignment, and positions Pure for a seamless transition into implementation and long‑term scalability.
