# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent and timely access to accurate information.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full client journey from lead generation to AUM conversion, enabling data‑driven decision‑making.  
- Enhance the Power BI user experience through intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and clear visualizations.  
- Strengthen pipeline and CAC tracking processes to improve transparency and operational efficiency.  
- Build a scalable, integrated data foundation that supports future growth and additional data sources across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric.
