# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting outputs.  
- Provide leadership with a clear, data‑driven understanding of the customer journey from lead generation to AUM growth and the efficiency of marketing spend.  
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring standardized KPIs and intuitive visualizations.  
- Strengthen pipeline and CAC tracking processes to support data maturity and scalable reporting capabilities aligned with Pure’s long‑term growth vision.  
- Ensure the data platform remains scalable and integrated with key systems including Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric to support future expansion and reporting needs.
