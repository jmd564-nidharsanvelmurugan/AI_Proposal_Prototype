# Understanding

- PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC seeks to address limitations in its existing pipeline reporting by developing detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting to improve visibility into marketing and conversion performance.  
- Current pain points include insufficient granularity in pipeline data and the absence of integrated CAC insights across systems.  
- The firm requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model capable of supporting comprehensive Power BI reporting and data quality assessment.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to streamline L2A and CAC reporting processes.  
- The system will be used by data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams to analyze and interpret performance metrics.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete data coverage for pipeline and cost reporting.  
- Security and compliance requirements are not specified in the available documentation.
