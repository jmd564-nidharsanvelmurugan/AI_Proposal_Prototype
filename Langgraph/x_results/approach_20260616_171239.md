# Approach

The engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific need for enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting within its SaaS‑based financial application environment. Operating under a B2C model, Pure’s business depends on understanding the full customer journey—from lead generation through to assets under management—and optimizing marketing ROI. This approach leverages the existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure while integrating new data sources such as QuickBooks to deliver a unified, automated, and scalable reporting solution.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased framework that ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities, technical dependencies, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—from discovery and design through implementation planning—providing clear checkpoints for validation and refinement. This disciplined progression ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with Pure’s growth objectives.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the L2A and CAC reporting solution through data assessment, model design, and visualization planning.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm reporting priorities and success metrics.  
- Assess existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for completeness and quality.  
- Design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model aligned with business logic and KPI definitions.  
- Develop Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize key metrics and validate reporting requirements.  
- Perform a data gap assessment and recommend remediation or enrichment strategies.  
- Document technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Translate the design outputs into a detailed implementation plan and prepare for execution of the data model and reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Finalize the L2A and CAC data model based on stakeholder feedback and data quality findings.  
- Define transformation logic and batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining sequencing, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Validate integration pathways for Salesforce and QuickBooks, ensuring secure and reliable data flow.  
- Refine Power BI mock‑ups into production‑ready dashboard specifications.  
- Provide recommendations for ongoing data governance and scalability to support future reporting needs.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing from discovery through implementation planning, the engagement provides a transparent roadmap for achieving automated, insightful, and scalable L2A and CAC reporting aligned with stakeholder expectations.
