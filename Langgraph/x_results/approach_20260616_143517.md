# Approach

The engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) pipeline. Operating within the **financial advisory and wealth management** industry and following a **B2C** model, Pure’s initiative focuses on enhancing data integration and reporting maturity across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks. The approach leverages existing infrastructure while introducing refined data modeling and automated batch pipelines to deliver scalable, insight‑driven reporting that supports marketing ROI analysis and leadership decision‑making.

A structured, phased methodology ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities, technical dependencies, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds logically on the previous one—from discovery and data assessment through model design and implementation planning—providing a clear roadmap for execution. This disciplined progression enables efficient collaboration between data, technology, and marketing teams while maintaining focus on quality, automation, and actionable insights.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery and Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the L2A and CAC reporting solution through data discovery, source assessment, and initial model and dashboard design.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff and stakeholder alignment sessions to confirm scope, access, and success criteria.  
- Review existing data architecture and documentation across Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI.  
- Assess data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for completeness and quality.  
- Define KPI logic and calculation methodology for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority reporting use cases.  
- Identify data gaps and recommend remediation or enrichment strategies for Phase 3B.

---

**Phase 3B: Build and Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Finalize the data model design, confirm technical approach, and prepare the implementation roadmap for automated reporting and integration.

**Activities:**  
- Validate and finalize the end‑state L2A and CAC data model based on stakeholder feedback and mock‑up iterations.  
- Design automated batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Develop technical approach for QuickBooks integration and ensure seamless connectivity with Salesforce.  
- Conduct data quality assessment and define cleaning/enrichment recommendations.  
- Produce the KPI Book, Data Model design documentation, and Technical Approach deliverables.  
- Create the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build steps, dependencies, and sequencing for rollout.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing from discovery through design and implementation planning, the engagement provides a robust foundation for scalable, automated, and insight‑rich reporting aligned with stakeholder expectations and long‑term growth objectives.
