# Outcomes

This engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by enabling data‑driven visibility into the firm’s Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The solution aligns with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and supports both management and private equity stakeholders in optimizing marketing ROI and growth efficiency through accurate, automated reporting.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility and Conversion Insights:** The new L2A data model will provide end‑to‑end transparency from lead generation to AUM realization, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate and Automated CAC Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks financial data with Salesforce and Tamarac will enable precise tracking of marketing spend and acquisition cost, improving decision‑making around budget allocation and campaign effectiveness.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Unified reporting across data sources will quantify the return on marketing investments, helping stakeholders assess which channels and tactics deliver the highest AUM growth relative to spend.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual reporting effort and ensure consistent, timely updates to Power BI dashboards for all business users.  
- **Enhanced Data‑Driven Leadership Decisions:** With reliable L2A and CAC KPIs visualized in Power BI, senior leadership will gain actionable insights to guide strategic planning, resource prioritization, and long‑term growth initiatives.  
- **Foundation for Scalable Data Maturity:** Establishing a robust, integrated data platform will position Pure for future expansion of analytics capabilities, supporting additional data sources and advanced performance metrics as the business evolves.
