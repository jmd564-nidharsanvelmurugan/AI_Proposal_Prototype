# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey—from lead generation through to assets under management—to inform strategic decision‑making.  
- Enhance the Power BI user experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and visually clear performance metrics.  
- Strengthen pipeline and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to improve transparency and accountability across marketing and sales functions.  
- Build a scalable data and reporting foundation capable of supporting future data sources, expanded analytics, and long‑term data maturity for PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC.
