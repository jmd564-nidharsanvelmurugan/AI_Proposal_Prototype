# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting outputs.  
- Provide leadership with a clear, data‑driven understanding of the customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management.  
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.  
- Strengthen pipeline and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to support data‑driven decision‑making and business growth.  
- Build a scalable reporting foundation capable of integrating future data sources and supporting long‑term data maturity across the organization.
