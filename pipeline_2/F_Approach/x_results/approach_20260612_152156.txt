# Approach

The engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) pipeline. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s growth depends on understanding the full customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management. This approach leverages the company’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure to design and implement a scalable, automated reporting solution that integrates Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data sources.

Our structured, phased methodology ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives and technical environment. Each phase builds progressively—from data access and discovery through to model design and implementation planning—providing clear checkpoints for stakeholder engagement and validation. This disciplined approach enables rapid progress within defined timelines while maintaining flexibility to adapt to data quality findings and integration dependencies.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Access Enablement, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish foundational access, documentation, and workshop cadence to enable efficient project execution and ensure all prerequisites are met before design activities begin.

**Activities:**  
- Coordinate with internal stakeholders to secure access to Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks environments.  
- Gather existing data architecture, infrastructure, and reporting documentation.  
- Schedule and confirm workshops and meeting cadences with data, technology, and marketing teams.  
- Review existing reporting outputs and reconciliation sources to ensure alignment with prior phases.  
- Validate readiness for data ingestion and confirm dependencies for QuickBooks integration.

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Model Definition, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Develop a deep understanding of current data sources and define the target‑state L2A and CAC data model to support enhanced reporting capabilities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct detailed assessment of Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data structures.  
- Map data flows and identify gaps affecting L2A and CAC reporting granularity.  
- Evaluate data quality and availability, providing recommendations for improvement.  
- Define KPI logic and data relationships for the L2A and CAC model.  
- Develop high‑level Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority metrics and validate design direction.  
- Identify “no‑regret” moves and quick wins for early implementation.

---

**Phase 3: Solution Design & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 4**

**Summary:** Finalize the data model design, confirm technical approach for integrations, and produce a detailed implementation roadmap for Phase 3B execution.

**Activities:**  
- Refine and finalize the L2A and CAC data model based on stakeholder feedback and mock‑up validation.  
- Develop technical approach for automated batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Define integration method for QuickBooks and confirm data reconciliation processes.  
- Document data cleaning and enrichment recommendations derived from prior assessments.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build activities, dependencies, and sequencing.  
- Deliver the KPI Book, Data Model Design, and Technical Approach documentation.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC**’s data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing through discovery, assessment, and design in a disciplined manner, the engagement provides a solid foundation for scalable, automated, and insight‑driven reporting aligned with stakeholder expectations.
