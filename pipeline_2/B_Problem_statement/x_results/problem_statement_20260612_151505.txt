# Problem Statement

PURE Financial Advisors LLC currently faces limitations in its data and reporting environment that hinder accurate visibility into marketing and pipeline performance. Existing Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting lacks the necessary granularity to trace the full journey from lead generation through to assets under management. Data required for these insights is dispersed across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks, with integration gaps—particularly around QuickBooks ingestion—creating inconsistencies and incomplete views of acquisition costs. The absence of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines further constrains timely and reliable reporting.

These technical shortcomings translate into significant business challenges. Leadership and marketing teams lack a consolidated, trustworthy view of pipeline performance and marketing ROI, limiting their ability to evaluate conversion effectiveness and optimize spend. Without integrated CAC insights and granular L2A tracking, decision‑makers cannot confidently assess which marketing activities drive the most value or identify bottlenecks in the acquisition funnel. This fragmented reporting landscape reduces confidence in performance metrics and impedes data‑driven growth planning across the organization.
