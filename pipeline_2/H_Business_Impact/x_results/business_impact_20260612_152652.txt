# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes granular Lead‑to‑AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting, enabling Pure Financial Advisors to quantify marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automates data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60% and improving reporting timeliness.  
- **Data‑Driven Decision Making:** Provides leadership with accurate, unified insights into pipeline performance, empowering faster, evidence‑based decisions on lead conversion strategies and asset growth initiatives.  
- **Scalable Reporting Architecture:** Builds a future‑ready data model within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, ensuring scalability for new data sources and supporting Pure’s long‑term data maturity roadmap.  
- **Improved Financial Transparency:** Integrates financial data from QuickBooks to deliver precise CAC metrics, strengthening accountability and enabling more accurate forecasting of acquisition efficiency.  
- **Stakeholder Alignment and Confidence:** Creates a single source of truth for marketing and financial performance, fostering trust among senior leadership and investment partners such as Lee Equity Partners.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, driving sustained growth, improved profitability, and long‑term value creation within the competitive U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector.
