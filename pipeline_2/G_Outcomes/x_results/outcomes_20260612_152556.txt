# Outcomes

This engagement with **PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by enabling data‑driven visibility into the firm’s Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. Focused on pipeline analytics and marketing ROI optimization, the initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C growth model and the strategic objectives of both senior leadership and its investment partner, Lee Equity Partners, to strengthen decision‑making through accurate, automated reporting.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility and Conversion Insights:** The new L2A data model will provide end‑to‑end transparency into the customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate and Automated CAC Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will produce reliable, automated CAC reporting, allowing marketing teams to assess true cost‑to‑acquire and improve return on marketing spend.  
- **Improved Data‑Driven Decision Making:** Unified reporting across Power BI will empower executives with consistent, actionable insights into pipeline performance, supporting strategic allocation of marketing and operational resources.  
- **Enhanced Reporting Efficiency and Consistency:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting processes, ensuring timely refreshes and reducing the risk of data discrepancies.  
- **Scalable Foundation for Future Analytics Growth:** The solution will establish a robust, extensible data architecture within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, positioning Pure for continued data maturity and integration of future data sources.  
- **Reduced Risk of Marketing Inefficiency:** By addressing current visibility gaps, the firm will mitigate the risk of underperforming campaigns and misaligned spend, ensuring marketing investments are directly linked to measurable AUM growth.
