**Deliverables**

The following deliverables are expected from the discovery phase:

1. **KPI Book**
	* Description: A comprehensive document outlining the key performance indicators (KPIs) for the client, including metrics that matter and their calculation methodology.
	* Format: Excel or Word
	* Key components: List of priority KPIs, data points required, proposed calculation methodology, and target dimensions for slicing the business.
	* [TO BE ENRICHED with Problem Statement] to ensure alignment with client objectives
2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
	* Description: Limited to 8 views, these mock-ups will provide a visual representation of the insights and reports for the priority metrics, capturing layout, structure, and functionality requirements.
	* Format: Power BI
	* Key components: Dashboard design, layout, and functionality requirements
	* [TO BE ENRICHED with Objectives] to ensure alignment with client goals
3. **Data Model Design**
	* Description: A high-level, conceptual data model outlining the structure and relationships between data entities.
	* Format: Word or PowerPoint
	* Key components: Entity-relationship diagram, data entity descriptions, and data flow diagrams
	* [TO BE ENRICHED with Business Context] to ensure alignment with industry and client context
4. **Data Gap Assessment**
	* Description: An assessment of data availability for the client cube build and reporting, identifying gaps in coverage and providing recommendations for process improvement.
	* Format: Word or PowerPoint
	* Key components: Data availability assessment, gap analysis, and process improvement recommendations
	* [TO BE ENRICHED with Problem Statement] to ensure alignment with client pain points
5. **Technical Approach**
	* Description: A document outlining the technical approach for the QuickBooks integration, including data preparation and visualization tools.
	* Format: Word or PowerPoint
	* Key components: Technical architecture, data preparation and visualization tools, and integration approach
	* [TO BE ENRICHED with Business Context] to ensure alignment with industry and client context
6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
	* Description: A plan outlining the implementation approach for the next phase, including timelines, milestones, and resource allocation.
	* Format: Word or PowerPoint
	* Key components: Implementation timeline, milestones, resource allocation, and risk management plan
	* [TO BE ENRICHED with Objectives] to ensure alignment with client goals
