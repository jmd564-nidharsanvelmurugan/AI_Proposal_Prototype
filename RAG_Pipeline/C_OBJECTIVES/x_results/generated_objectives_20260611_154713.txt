PURE Financial Advisors LLC aims to establish a unified, data‑driven foundation for understanding and optimizing its client acquisition and asset growth performance. The future‑state vision centers on achieving full transparency across the Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, enabling leadership to make informed, evidence‑based decisions about marketing effectiveness and business growth. By creating a single, consistent view of the client journey—from initial lead generation through to managed assets—PURE will be able to measure conversion efficiency, evaluate return on marketing spend, and align investment decisions with measurable business outcomes. This enhanced visibility will strengthen strategic planning and support the firm’s broader goal of scaling its wealth management operations with precision and accountability.  

Technically, the objective is to deliver an integrated and scalable data model that consolidates information from Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric into a cohesive reporting environment. This model will serve as the single source of truth for pipeline and acquisition performance, ensuring that all stakeholders—from marketing to senior leadership—are working from consistent, validated data. Automated data ingestion and transformation processes will underpin this environment, reducing manual effort and improving data reliability. The resulting Power BI reporting suite will provide intuitive, KPI‑driven dashboards that present granular insights into lead conversion, cost efficiency, and marketing ROI, supporting both operational and strategic decision‑making.  

Operationally, PURE seeks to embed automation and governance into its reporting processes, ensuring that data refreshes, transformations, and quality checks occur seamlessly and consistently. This will not only improve reporting timeliness but also elevate data maturity across the organization, positioning PURE to scale its analytics capabilities as new data sources and business needs emerge. In line with best practices observed across leading financial services firms, the initiative will establish a standardized reporting framework and KPI definitions that promote transparency, comparability, and confidence in performance metrics. Ultimately, the future‑state solution will empower PURE Financial Advisors to operate with greater analytical rigor, enabling sustained growth and enhanced value creation for both clients and stakeholders.
