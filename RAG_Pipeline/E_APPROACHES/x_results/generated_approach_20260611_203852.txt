## 3. Approach

Our approach to designing and building the L2A and CAC data model, reporting suite, and QuickBooks integration for Pure Financial Advisors is structured into phases, ensuring a logical flow from understanding the requirements to delivering the solution.

### Phase 1: Assessment and Planning (Weeks 1-3)
**Duration:** 3 weeks

**Key Activities:**
- Conduct a KPI definition workshop with marketing and finance teams to establish clear and consistent KPI definitions for L2A and CAC reporting.
- Perform a data gap assessment to identify existing data quality issues and gaps in the current reporting process.
- Develop a detailed project plan, including timelines, milestones, and resource allocation.
- Conduct a technical assessment of the current Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse data platforms to understand their capabilities and limitations.

**Stakeholders Involved:**
- Data and technology stakeholders
- Senior leadership
- Marketing teams
- Finance teams

**Outputs:**
- KPI Definition Book
- Data Gap Assessment Report
- Project Plan

### Phase 2: Design (Weeks 4-6)
**Duration:** 3 weeks

**Key Activities:**
- Design the L2A and CAC data model, incorporating the KPI definitions and addressing data quality issues identified during the assessment phase.
- Create Power BI mock-ups for the 8-page reporting suite, ensuring alignment with the designed data model and stakeholder requirements.
- Develop a technical approach document outlining the integration of QuickBooks with the existing Azure Fabric/Synapse data platform.
- Conduct workshops with stakeholders to review and refine the data model and Power BI mock-ups.

**Stakeholders Involved:**
- Data and technology stakeholders
- Senior leadership
- Marketing teams
- Finance teams

**Outputs:**
- Data Model Design
- Power BI Mock-ups
- Technical Approach Document

### Phase 3A: QuickBooks Ingestion and Data Quality Assessment (Weeks 7-9)
**Duration:** 3 weeks

**Key Activities:**
- Ingest data from QuickBooks into the Azure Fabric/Synapse data platform.
- Perform a thorough data quality assessment to ensure the ingested data meets the requirements for L2A and CAC reporting.
- Develop and implement data remediation plans for any identified data quality issues.

**Stakeholders Involved:**
- Data and technology stakeholders
- Finance teams

**Outputs:**
- QuickBooks Data Ingestion Report
- Data Quality Assessment Report

### Phase 3B: Implementation and Testing (Weeks 10-18)
**Duration:** 8-12 weeks

**Key Activities:**
- Implement the designed data model and integrate QuickBooks with the Azure Fabric/Synapse data platform.
- Develop and deploy the Power BI reporting suite.
- Conduct thorough testing of the integrated system, including data validation and report functionality.
- Provide training to end-users on the new reporting suite and integrated system.

**Stakeholders Involved:**
- Data and technology stakeholders
- Senior leadership
- Marketing teams
- Finance teams
- End-users

**Outputs:**
- Implemented Data Model
- Integrated QuickBooks with Azure Fabric/Synapse
- Deployed Power BI Reporting Suite
- Training Documentation
